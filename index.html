    How it is simulated

    Everything runs in your browser; images are never uploaded. Colours are converted to linear RGB, transformed, then converted back to sRGB.

    • Complete red, green and blue loss: Brettel, Viénot & Mollon, 1997.
    • Partial red and green loss: Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes, 2009.
    • Partial blue loss: Brettel 1997 blended with the original by severity.
    • Monochromacy: relative luminance (Rec. 709).
    • Constants follow the DaltonLens reference.
